# Reducing Digital Clutter

I've decided that I already have enough computers to babysit and so I've deco'd my horrifically out of date (Debian 8) VPS and moved stuff onto someone elses computer. 

Of course this has meant relocating a bunch of services like influxdb and grafana somewhere, that somewhere being a NAS in the cupboard... so I have exactly the same number of computers to babysit but at least this one is 'Free™' and marginally less exposed to the Internet. I don't think that will count for much when everything breaks and I have to fix it (inevitably on a Sunday night) but progress is progress.
